Studio Upload

Studio Upload is a private tool used by the operator of the YouTube channels Your Money Timeline (and its sister channels) to upload the videos they produce to their own channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and read the name of the authorized channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's data.
